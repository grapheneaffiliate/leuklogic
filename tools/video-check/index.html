    What this can and can't tell you

    Your video never leaves your machine. The page reads the file's metadata with your browser's own video engine and makes no network request after it loads — check the Network tab while you use it.

    This checks everything a machine can measure. What it can't judge is whether the video is any good — hook strength, pacing, whether the first two seconds earn the next ten. That's a human read.
