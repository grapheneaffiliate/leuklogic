What I won't do

Small and fast has edges. Here they are.

  • I'm one person. If you need a team, a signed SLA and 24/7 on-call, I'm the wrong call and I'll tell you in the first email.
  • I don't build mobile apps.
  • I don't do six-month platform rebuilds, or take over someone else's abandoned codebase sight unseen.
  • I won't sell you AI for a problem that needs a checklist. Sometimes the honest answer is “that's a $9/month tool.”
  • I don't do retainers. If nothing needs building, you shouldn't be paying me.

From clients

Why is this cheaper and faster than an agency?

No account manager, no bench, no office. One person who leans on AI tooling for the typing and keeps the architecture, the review and the responsibility. You're paying for the work, not the org chart.

How is a bot only a few hundred dollars?

Because it's a few days of tightly-scoped work, and I'm not padding the estimate against scope creep. Agreeing the scope up front is what makes the low number safe for both of us.

What if it takes longer than you said?

The price doesn't move. If the scope moves, we agree on a new number before anything else happens.

Do you maintain it afterwards?

Per change, if you want it. Nothing is required. It's your code and any competent developer can pick it up — that's deliberate.
