Questions people actually ask

Why is this cheaper and faster than an agency?

No account manager, no bench, no office. One person who leans on AI tooling for the typing and keeps the architecture, the review and the responsibility. You're paying for the work, not the org chart.

Because it's a few days of tightly-scoped work, and I'm not padding the estimate against scope creep. Agreeing the scope up front is what makes the low number safe for both of us.
