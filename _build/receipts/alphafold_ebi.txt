Background AlphaFold is an AI system developed by Google DeepMind that predicts a protein’s 3D structure from its amino acid sequence. It regularly achieves accuracy competitive with experiment. Google DeepMind and EMBL’s European Bioinformatics Institute ( EMBL-EBI ) have partnered to create AlphaFold DB to make these predictions freely available to the scientific community. The latest database release contains over 200 million entries, providing broad coverage of UniProt (the standard repository of protein sequences and annotations). We provide individual downloads for the human proteome and for the proteomes of 47 other key organisms important in research and global health. We also provide a download for the manually curated subset of UniProt ( Swiss-Prot ). Q8I3H7: May protect the malaria parasite against attack by the immune system. Mean pLDDT 85.57. AI-derived residue annotations – October 2026 AlphaFold Database now integrates AI-derived residue-level annotations from the scientific literature, providing biological context for individual residues alongside predicted protein structures. A transformer-based named entity recognition (NER) model identifies protein residues and point mutations in structural biology publications and links experimentally supported annotations to UniProt reference sequences. Annotations are transferred from experimentally validated PDB structures to AlphaFold models using the SIFTS framework, ensuring that literature-derived residue annotations are supported by experimental evidence. This release enables users to navigate directly from residues in AlphaFold predictions to publications describing their function, interactions, and experimentally observed mutations, making it easier to interpret predicted structures in their biological context.
