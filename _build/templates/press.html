The mission

In one paragraph.

Abundance for All is a mission site published by LeukLogic. It argues that we should build superintelligence and robotics to provide all needs and reasonable wants for humanity: not to replace people, but to free them to raise families, create, serve one another and rest. It pairs the idea with sourced data on where the world falls short today, a short film, and a free card maker, "Design your abundant day", that lets anyone say what they would do with their time back.

Short version: We believe we should build superintelligence and robotics to provide every need, and every reasonable want, for every person.

Contact: tim@leuklogic.com. LeukLogic is a US software company run by Tim McGirl.
