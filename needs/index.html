Every human need

Where the gaps are today, and what could close them.

One real number for each of six needs, with its year, its source and its licence. These describe the world now. They are not predictions, and they are the reason we think the work is worth doing.

Explore the six needs

Food

8.5%of people undernourished

8.5% of the world's people were undernourished in 2023.

What we believe could change

We believe robots that plant, tend and harvest, guided by a superintelligence that learns every field, could help every harvest reach every table.

Water

73.7%of people used safely managed drinking water

73.7% of people used safely managed drinking water in 2024. About one person in four did not.

What we believe could change

We believe tireless machines and smarter engineering could build, repair and watch over the pipes, pumps and treatment plants that safe water depends on.

48.3%of 10-year-olds below minimum reading level

48.3% of children at the end of primary-school age were below minimum reading proficiency in 2019 (learning poverty).

What we believe could change

We believe a patient AI tutor in every child's own language could be there for every learner, wherever the nearest school is.

The three ingredients

Work, energy and knowing how.

Behind every need are the same three things. Here is one real, current fact for each.

  • Work. The world's factories now run a record 5 million industrial robots, up 9% in 2025.Source: International Federation of Robotics (press release, Sep 24, 2026), 2025. Quoted fact from an IFR press release (Sep 24, 2026); © IFR, linked and attributed, not reproduced.
  • Energy. Solar produced 8.7% of the world's electricity in 2025.Source: Ember, via Our World in Data, 2025. CC BY 4.0 (Ember; shown via Our World in Data).
  • Knowing how. An AI system's predicted protein structures are now free to every scientist: over 200 million of them.Source: EMBL-EBI AlphaFold Protein Structure Database, 2026. CC BY 4.0 (EMBL-EBI AlphaFold Protein Structure Database).
